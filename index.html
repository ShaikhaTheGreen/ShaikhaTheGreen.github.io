About

A short bio.

I'm a faculty member in the Department of Information Sciences at Kuwait University, College of Life Sciences. My doctoral training is from Penn State (PSU), where my work spanned scientific machine learning and physics-informed neural networks.

My current research sits across three threads: AI safety (detecting prompt injection and jailbreak attempts on large language models), AI for cybersecurity (phishing detection, network anomaly detection, real-time threat monitoring), and scientific machine learning (PINNs and sparse identification of dynamical systems).

In the classroom I teach foundational courses in IT infrastructure and computer organization, and I build interactive tools — like Device Anatomy — to make hardware tangible for students.

ISC230 · Course

IT Infrastructure

Foundational course on computer organization and IT infrastructure, aligned to Tanenbaum & Austin's Structured Computer Organization (6th ed.). Covers the levels of a computer, processors and memory hierarchy, secondary storage, I/O, digital logic, and pipelining.
